The Problem

Tenable ships tens of thousands of plugins. Finding the exact one — by ID, name, or family — and seeing what it covers shouldn't require leaving your workflow for the platform search.

What It Does

Foxhound is a read-only search over every distinct Tenable plugin in navi.db. Query by plugin ID, name, or family to find the detection you're after, deduplicated to the plugin itself.

Key Capabilities

  • Search by ID, name, or family — one box over every distinct plugin in navi.db.
  • Distinct plugin list — deduplicated, so you see the detection, not thousands of finding rows.
  • Instant local results — runs against the navi.db mirror.
  • Read-only by design — Foxhound only searches.

How It Works

Foxhound runs a read-only search over the distinct plugins in your local navi.db and returns the matches.

Who It's For

Analysts identifying a plugin by ID or family; detection engineers checking what's in the catalog; anyone exploring plugins in their own data.
