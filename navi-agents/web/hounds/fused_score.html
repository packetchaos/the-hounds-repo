“Fuse every hound's signal into one score per asset.”
Nemesis, the goddess of retribution who brings the reckoning to what has earned it — here she fuses every hound's signal into one exposure score per asset, and ranks what to act on first.

The Problem

Each hound finds something real — KEV, EOL, weak crypto, an attack path, no owner — but a team can't work fifteen lists. The asset that appears on five of them matters more than any single finding, and no single score shows that.

What It Does

Nemesis gives every asset one 0–100 Exposure Score built from 16 signals — exploitability (KEV and EPSS), Tenable AES and VPR, ACR, attack paths, identity, compliance, web-app findings, EOL, internet exposure, AI risk, weak or expiring crypto, SLA aging, finding volume, ownership and ATT&CK — then shows what to act on now, the worst asset from each hound, and where their signals converge.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • 16 signals, one number — weights sum to 100, so a score reads as the share of the most danger one asset can carry; dispositions discount it, and Chronos's coverage flags how far to trust it.
  • Your weighting — drag any weight to re-rank the estate to what matters to you; each weighting is cached separately.
  • Act now — the assets over 80, 60 or 40 today, with one-click hand-offs: tag Priority, tag owners (Terminus), email owners (Gabriel), raise ACR (Anubis) or add to the Contract.
  • Worst from each hound — the top asset each hound's signal points at — and the assets where several converge.
  • Explained — every asset's score broken down by factor, and how widespread each signal is across the estate.
  • Priority tags — Priority : P1 to P4 on the act-now set, recomputed on the server from navi.db — not taken from the browser.

How It Works

Direct signals (KEV, EPSS, VPR, ACR, EOL, certificates, compliance, WAS, SLA aging) come straight from your local navi.db; the rest are read from the tags other hounds write. Per-asset aggregation runs inside the database, so it holds up on very large estates. Results are cached per navi.db and weighting, and rebuilt in the background.

Who It's For

VM leads who need one ranked list, and executives who want to know which assets carry the most danger — and why.

Why It's Different

  • A rank, not a count — the same ordering at 100 assets or a million.
  • Shows its work — every point traced to the hound and finding behind it.
  • Honest about blind spots — low scan coverage lowers confidence instead of hiding risk.
