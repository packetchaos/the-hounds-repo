The hunter from the underworld — Hellhound digs through every finding in your navi.db on command.

The Problem

Finding the assets behind a specific plugin, CVE, or severity means wrestling the platform's filters. You want a direct query over the findings navi already holds, in seconds.

What It Does

Hellhound is a read-only search over the vulnerabilities in navi.db. Query by plugin name, plugin ID, CVE, or severity and get the matching findings back, grouped by plugin so prevalence is obvious.

Key Capabilities

  • Search by plugin name, ID, CVE, or severity — one box over every finding in navi.db.
  • Grouped by plugin — results roll up so you see how widely a finding spreads at a glance.
  • Instant local results — runs against the navi.db mirror, not the API.
  • Read-only by design — Hellhound only searches.

How It Works

Hellhound runs a read-only search over the vulns in your local navi.db and returns matching findings grouped by plugin.

Who It's For

Analysts hunting the assets behind a CVE or plugin; responders scoping a specific finding; anyone querying the estate's vulnerabilities fast.
