"One asset — the whole story, in one dossier."
Odysseus's faithful hound — Argos recognizes a single asset across every table in navi.db and tells you everything the pack knows about it.

The Problem

When an asset matters — an incident, an audit, a crown jewel — you need its whole story in one place: risk, tags, CVEs, software, certificates. Instead it's scattered across a dozen tables and tools, and assembling it by hand costs the time you don't have when it counts most.

What It Does

Give Argos a single asset — a UUID or an IP — and it assembles one dossier: identity, a 0–100 risk score and band, every tag deciphered (what it means and which hound raised it), top CVEs, findings, software, and certificates with weak crypto flagged. One lookup, the complete picture.

Key Capabilities

  • Full identity card — hostname, IP, OS, network, ACR/AES, UUID, and a Tenable deep-link, assembled from every table at once.
  • 0–100 risk score + band — from the severity mix, KEV/exploitable findings, and the pack's tag intelligence, with a full severity breakdown.
  • Every tag deciphered — what each tag means and which hound raised it (Laelaps → KEV, Certania → cert failure, Charon → EOL, and the rest).
  • CVEs, findings, software & certs — top findings, installed software, and certificates with weak crypto flagged, all on one page.
  • Live navi explore uuid — runs the flag-driven per-asset views (software, patches, details, CVEs) through the navi CLI on demand.

How It Works

Argos reads navi.db read-only, recognizing one asset across every table into a single dossier; the Live panel runs navi explore uuid on demand. A deep-link (page.html?target=<uuid|ip>) auto-runs the lookup.

Who It's For

Incident responders who need one asset's full story fast; analysts triaging a specific host; anyone auditing a crown-jewel asset before a decision.

Proof Points
