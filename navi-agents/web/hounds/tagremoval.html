“Sweep out tags that no longer belong — deliberately, on the record.”
Garmr, the blood-stained hound at the gates of Hel who howls at Ragnarök, the world's cleansing — here he unmakes tags, clearing what no longer belongs before the pack re-tags.

The Problem

Tag estates rot: abandoned experiments, superseded schemes, dated tags that no longer apply. Tags drive scans, automation and reporting, so a stale tag quietly corrupts everything built on it — and the pack removes tags in one place only.

What It Does

Garmr lists every tag navi can remove — the tags in navi.db with the number of assets carrying each — and removes the ones you choose through the same background queue every hound uses. He also explains why some tags in Tenable are out of navi's reach, and can hand your choices to the Contract to clear at the start of every cycle before re-tagging.

At a glance

Key Capabilities

  • Only what navi can remove — the tags navi.db knows about, with asset counts — a tag that exists only in Tenable is shown separately, never offered.
  • Tags navi can't reach — navi.db assets compared with Tenable's licensed and stale assets.
  • Deliberate removal — pick the tags, confirm, and each is queued as a removal — marked as a removal in the Tagging log.
  • The pack's only remover — every other hound only adds tags; removal lives here and in the Contract's removal phase.
  • Hand-off to the Contract — the Contract removes your chosen tags first, pauses (30 minutes by default) for Tenable, runs navi update, then re-tags — without deleting the tags themselves.

How It Works

Garmr reads the tags table in your local navi.db and removes with navi enrich tag … -remove, which strips the category : value from the assets navi.db says carry it. Removals go behind the write gate and a confirm.

Who It's For

Tag governance owners and security operations teams who need the tag estate to stay meaningful as it grows.

Works Better With

Basset shows who carries a tag before you remove it, Covenant turns Garmr's list into a recurring clean-and-re-tag cycle, and NO : UPDATE protection set by Anubis can only be removed here.

Why It's Different

  • One place to remove — a single, logged path for every tag removal in the pack.
  • Clean, then re-tag — removal followed by fresh tagging keeps dated tags current without deleting the tag itself.
  • Nothing silent — every removal is confirmed and recorded.
