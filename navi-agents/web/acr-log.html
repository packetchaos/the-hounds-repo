A durable registry of every Asset Criticality Rating change the Hounds made — one row per asset per change: when, which hound (acr · contract · ai · mcp), the tag it targeted, the ACR before → after, the business case (Change Reasons + justification) and the result. Assets navi skipped are recorded too: skipped_protected means the asset carries NO : UPDATE and navi left its ACR alone; skipped_no_acr means navi.db had no ACR for it. blocked rows were never sent (writes are gated).
