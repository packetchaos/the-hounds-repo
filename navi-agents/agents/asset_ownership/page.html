📌 Asset Ownership

Ownership from the asset's point of view: who owns each asset, how much of it is claimed, what isn't owned, and what you're blind to — plus your crown jewels (ACR ≥ 9) and what the Hounds already know about them. Three layers: Business Owner (leaders own groups/apps), Owner (Atlas — routes & paths), and a single Asset Owner who owns an asset's every route & path (or just the rest).

Press ▶ Run

Rolls up ownership for every asset in navi.db — the three owner layers, crown jewels, blind spots and who owns what. Your last run is kept on the server, so coming back restores it instantly (and 🐾 Release the Hounds can run it for you).

📊 At a glance

Every number opens what it counts — a filter on the per-asset table below (assign an owner right there), a section of this page, or the hound that owns the data (Exposure Routes, Argos, Exposure Score, SLA Adherence).

✨ Ask — tag owners in plain English

Describe who owns which assets. The processor reads each asset's tags and attributes (e.g. Business Tier, Environment, ACR, crown-jewel). A group assignment writes a Business Owner; “complete / single / every route & path / the rest” writes a single Asset Owner that closes the asset.

👑 Crown jewels — what the Hounds know

Your most business-critical assets (ACR ≥ 9) — is each one owned, and what have the other Hounds already found on it?

🕳 Unclaimed & blind spots — what am I blind to?

Assets on the attack surface with no owner — crown jewels and high-ACR first. 🏷️ Tag owner inline, or tag one owner on all shown at once. Tagging writes an Asset Owner tag and cascades Owner tags across the asset's routes & paths (gated).
