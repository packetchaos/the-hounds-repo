🚨 Alerting

Ravens ride with the wolf pack — scouting ahead and calling out where the danger is. Alert Raven watches navi.db for conditions worth an email. Alerts fire on change (once, until they clear), evaluation runs in the AI Contract loop, and delivery is a gated digest. Detection is read-only.

✨ Ask — craft an alert

Describe it in plain English. Alert Raven searches the fields that define an asset — tag category & tag value, and navi's purpose-built tables: software, certs, routes, paths, and plugins (which navi pre-parses from plugin output, so they're faster and cleaner than scanning raw output). Join conditions with and to intersect — only assets matching all of them count.

e.g. "alert me on assets that have a MITRE and CISA KEV and EOL and NHI tag" · "assets running OpenSSL 1.0" · "certs signed with SHA-1" · "assets with a file under /opt/tomcat" · "critical when new CISA KEV appears". It drafts a read-only query you can test before saving; works without a key too.
