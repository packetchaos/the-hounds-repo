Pythia

🧠 AI Inventory

Discovers AI/ML by content: the Tenable AI plugin family plus the software & cpes inventories, plugin output and network endpoints. Classifies each asset by role, flags exposed / unauthenticated endpoints, maps data egress (shadow vs sanctioned) and MITRE ATLAS, and checks each asset's ACR against its role. Every row has a 🔍 Why to inspect the evidence and dismiss false positives. Tags the assets via navi tag --query; writes are gated and tracked in the Tagging log.
