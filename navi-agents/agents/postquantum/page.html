⚛️ Post-Quantum Cipher Analysis

Heimdall finds the crypto that won't survive a quantum computer. RSA, ECC and DSA are broken by Shor's algorithm, whatever the key length. It reads certificates, TLS/SSH key exchange, OpenSSH/OpenSSL versions and Tenable's Post-Quantum plugins (277650 / 277652 / 277653), weights each asset by ACR and CISA KEV, and ranks the migration. It tags the assets under Post-Quantum, PQC Priority, PQC Risk, PQC Harvest-Now, PQC Transport and PQC Agility. Writes are gated and tracked in the Tagging log.
