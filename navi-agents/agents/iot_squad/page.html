IoT Discovery Squad

Discovery → Carrier harvest → Cross-term hunt → QA. Two human gates: IoT tag approval, new-detection approval.

The term pack is an optional, curated vocabulary of known IoT/OT vendors (Sonos, Hikvision, Siemens, Synology…). Off = hunt only your registry's own terms. On = also sweep carrier plugins for the whole pack, so a device you never defined (e.g. Sonos) can surface for the first time. A bigger authoritative list can replace it via IOT_TERM_PACK_PATH. Everything it surfaces is still gated below.
