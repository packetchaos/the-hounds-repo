🔐 Certificate Agent

Certania finds certificates that have expired, month by month over the last 12 months plus everything older, and those failing in the next 12 months. It tags the assets carrying them by exact expiry date Cert failure : <Mon>-<DD>-<YYYY>, and by certificate weakness Cert Issue : <plugin>. Reads navi.db; writes are gated and tracked in the Tagging log. For ad-hoc certificate questions, use the MCP.
